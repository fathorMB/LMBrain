# Experimental Wayfinder decision maps

Wayfinder is an opt-in shaping capability for a genuinely multi-session effort
with a clear destination and material unresolved route. It complements the
roadmap and ordinary `SPEC-*` lifecycle; it never replaces either.

Use it only after the operator agrees to chart a map. A map records a bounded
destination, active fog, and decision tickets. Tickets may be `conversation`,
`prototype`, `research`, or `prerequisite`; none authorize production code,
configuration, tests, or specialist dispatch. Conversation and prototype
resolutions must retain explicit operator evidence.

Artifacts live at stable paths in `.lmbrain/wayfinder/maps/` (`MAP-*`) and
`.lmbrain/wayfinder/tickets/` (`WAY-*`). Create, claim, resolve and clear them
only through the `wayfinder_*` MCP operations. A map clears only when its fog,
tickets and graph diagnostics are settled; then use the normal spec workflow.

The capability is experimental. Preserve evidence about context size, repeated
questions, rework and usability before proposing any change to roadmap policy.
Its inspiration is [Wayfinder](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/wayfinder/SKILL.md), under its upstream MIT terms.

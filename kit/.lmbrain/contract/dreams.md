# Operator-Invited Dreaming Capability Module

This module defines the exploratory ideation protocol for LMBrain workspaces.

## Scope & Application

Activated only when the human operator explicitly invites the Project Lead to rest, dream, or explore tentative ideas in conversation.

## Dream Capture Rules

- Operates strictly from current project context and artifacts.
- Captures tentative `DREAM-*` records under `knowledge/` or the dream store.
- Requires provenance, context digest, classification (`technical-debt` or `design-debt`), confidence, and suggested disposition.
- Dreams are never promoted automatically to specs, debts, or roadmap items.

## Dream Verbs

- `dream_capture`: Record a tentative grounded observation during an invited dream session.
